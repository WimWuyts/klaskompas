# Behaviour and Classroom Management Contract v1.0

**Status:** Phase 1B consolidation candidate  
**Sources:** school regulations 2025-2026 plus approved decisions 1B-D1 through D6  
**Purpose:** one stable classroom-management contract for English and Spanish lessons

## 1. Principles

KlasKompas supports a clear, humane and restorative classroom route:

```text
prevent → redirect → remind → internally record → apply announced response → follow up/consult
```

It is not a punishment counter and does not create automatic sanctions, risk scores or permanent pupil labels.

## 2. Six expectations

Dutch is mandatory and primary. English or Spanish may appear as a supporting subject-language line.

| No. | Dutch — primary | English — supporting | Spanish — supporting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start en eindig volgens de routine. | Start and finish with the routine. | Empieza y termina siguiendo la rutina. |
| 2 | Werk actief mee en volg de instructie. | Work actively and follow instructions. | Participa activamente y sigue las instrucciones. |
| 3 | Laat iedereen leren. | Let everyone learn. | Deja que todos aprendan. |
| 4 | Behandel iedereen respectvol en houd het veilig. | Treat everyone with respect and keep it safe. | Trata a todos con respeto y actúa de forma segura. |
| 5 | Gebruik materiaal en technologie zoals afgesproken. | Use materials and technology as agreed. | Usa el material y la tecnología según lo acordado. |
| 6 | Neem verantwoordelijkheid en herstel wat fout loopt. | Take responsibility and put things right. | Asume tu responsabilidad y repara lo ocurrido. |

Class settings:

- `nl`;
- `nl-en`;
- `nl-es`.

Formal response and official communication remain Dutch-first.

## 3. Ordinary response route

### L0 — prevention and routine

Visible expectations, assigned seating where useful, prepared material/start task, clear device rule, short checked instructions, taught transitions and closing routine.

**Stored:** no individual behaviour event.

### L1 — non-verbal redirect

Eye contact, proximity, gesture, pointing or pause.

**Stored:** normally no.

### L2 — brief private reminder

One calm factual sentence naming the action and required correction.

**Stored:** normally no.

### L3 — internal classroom record with announced response

Use when the same ordinary behaviour continues after a clear reminder or when a clear non-serious boundary requires an internal record.

It records:

- observable behaviour;
- expectation;
- required correction;
- announced approved L4 response;
- pupil/class/lesson/time/teacher;
- later correction/withdrawal and audit history.

Compact UI label: **Interne klasregistratie**.

Suggested Dutch sentence:

> “Dit gedrag is na de herinnering blijven doorgaan. Ik noteer dit voor mijn lesopvolging. Als dit opnieuw gebeurt, [aangekondigde klasreactie].”

L3 is teacher-private and local by default. It is not an official warning, agenda note, Smartschool/LVS record or order measure.

### L4 — announced direct classroom response

When the same behaviour repeats in the lesson, apply the response announced at L3 instead of issuing another warning.

Store outcome as:

- `completed`;
- `refused`;
- `adapted`;
- `withdrawn`.

### L5 — school-follow-up gate

Use when ordinary classroom management does not restore workable learning conditions, a pupil refuses L4, cross-lesson review warrants consultation, parent/formal follow-up is consciously needed or class removal is considered.

KlasKompas creates a proposal/request. It does not mark an official measure as imposed before authorised confirmation.

## 4. Approved L4 response map

| Behaviour category | Default response | Approved alternative |
|---|---|---|
| Start routine not followed | Complete the missed routine step correctly | Designated individual start place until started |
| Instruction disruption | Seat change to agreed individual position | Separate working position until calm re-entry |
| Task not started/off-task | Individual work position with defined task block/checkpoint | Equivalent paper task when digital distraction is causal |
| Peer/group disruption | Temporary individual work for the activity | Bounded alternative role or work position |
| Unauthorised smartphone use | Device into numbered classroom pouch for the remainder of the lesson | None in ordinary route |
| Unauthorised laptop use | Close device and complete equivalent paper task | Reposition screen/seat when digital access remains necessary |
| Reasonable instruction not followed | Individual work position and complete original instruction | Choice between two valid completion routes |
| Non-serious disrespect/provocation | Separate involved pupils and pause interaction | Temporary individual work |
| Transition/closing routine not followed | Redo missed step correctly | Next transition individually on teacher cue |
| Non-serious material/space misuse | Stop use and restore condition | Alternative material under direct supervision |

Formal order measures never appear as L4 alternatives.

## 5. Repeated behaviour across lessons

### P1

Three same-category L3/L4 records within the last six lesson contacts with the class require a short follow-up conversation.

The conversation:

1. names the factual pattern;
2. checks context and preventive support;
3. agrees one observable expectation;
4. decides whether school consultation is needed.

No automatic sanction or official warning follows.

### P2

A further same-category L3/L4 record within four lesson contacts after the conversation prompts review and, where appropriate, consultation with class teacher, behaviour coach or student-support route.

### Closure

The active pattern closes after:

- six consecutive class lesson contacts without recurrence;
- explicit successful teacher review;
- replacement by an official school process.

Categories remain separate. Lateness, assessment absence and serious incidents are excluded.

## 6. Serious route

The ordinary ladder and pattern counts are skipped for violence, credible threats, serious bullying, sexual-boundary violations, severe discriminatory/racist incidents, dangerous objects, drugs/alcohol, serious theft/damage or acute safety risk.

```text
stop behaviour
→ restore safety
→ summon authorised support
→ confirm handover
→ record factual observations
→ school-owned outcome
```

## 7. Smartphone rule

Every lesson begins in `prohibited` mode, including the two Spanish classes in the third stage.

A smartphone may be used only during a teacher-activated, bounded pedagogical exception:

```text
teacher_authorised_temporary_use
```

A visible stop action returns the lesson to `prohibited`.

Unauthorised use instruction:

> “Het toestel is nu niet toegestaan. Stop het in de genummerde smartphonehoes.”

Refusal:

```text
internal classroom record
→ reasonable instruction not followed
→ L5 school follow-up
```

KlasKompas invents no confiscation period and does not instruct the teacher to retain a device outside the authorised routine.

## 8. Internal versus official records

Keep separate:

- internal L3 source record;
- linked L4 response;
- conversation/repair action;
- parent-contact action;
- school-follow-up proposal;
- confirmed official school record;
- Smartschool/LVS publication attempt.

No internal record is silently promoted or published. The original source remains auditably preserved.

## 9. Language rule

- pupil expectations: Dutch first, optional English or Spanish below;
- L1/L2 prompts may use subject language;
- L3-L5 and official text: Dutch first;
- parent communication and Smartschool/LVS preview: Dutch by default.

## 10. Interface rule

L3 flow:

```text
select pupil
→ select observable category
→ review expectation and announced response
→ confirm internal record
```

L4 reopens the originating L3 and confirms the announced response/outcome. No second warning is created.

## 11. Safeguards

- no collective punishment;
- no public humiliation or forced public apology;
- no forced mediation;
- no personality/motivation labels;
- no loss of approved accommodation;
- no automatic detention, punishment work or removal;
- no combined score or permanent red/green identity;
- no Smartschool publication without rights, mapping, preview and explicit confirmation;
- correction and withdrawal remain audit events.

## 12. Superseded terminology

The phrase `documented classroom warning` in earlier drafts is deprecated. It must be migrated to `internal classroom record with announced response` before implementation, fixtures and tests are considered complete.
