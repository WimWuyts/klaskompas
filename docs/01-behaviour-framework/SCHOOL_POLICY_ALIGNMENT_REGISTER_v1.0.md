## 2. Product-owner requirements aligned with, but not fully specified by, the regulations

The following are product requirements rather than detailed school procedures:

### Personal overview

One private teacher dashboard combines open attendance, assessment, make-up, remediation, behaviour and other follow-up tasks. This supports organisation but does not replace official records.

### Manual remediation tracking

The school regulations support additional chances and learning from mistakes, but they do not define a KlasKompas remediation workflow. The project owner requires a limited, manually confirmed instructional remediation tracker.

KlasKompas therefore may store:

- factual instructional need/source;
- objective/action;
- due/review date;
- status and outcome.

It may not automatically assign remediation from grades, diagnose a problem or create an official care/support dossier.

### Cross-device realtime use

Laptop, tablet and phone share the teacher's canonical working state. This is an internal product behaviour and not Smartschool publication.

## 3. Approved classroom boundaries

### Teacher-private/internal

- attendance working deviations;
- ordinary assessment and make-up tracking;
- manually confirmed remediation;
- L3 internal classroom record;
- L4 direct response;
- short conversation and simple repair;
- personal follow-up task;
- draft parent/school follow-up.

### Separate official school route

- official attendance/legal justification;
- official examination scheduling;
- official warning or agenda/Smartschool note;
- punishment work as formal order measure;
- detention/study detention;
- formal service or class removal;
- behaviour contract/formal restorative process;
- disciplinary measures;
- official support/care dossier;
- official Smartschool/LVS publication.

## 4. Serious incidents excluded from ordinary counts

- violence or credible threat;
- serious bullying;
- sexual-boundary violation;
- severe discriminatory/racist incident;
- dangerous object;
- drugs/alcohol;
- serious theft or deliberate serious damage;
- acute safety risk.

These use immediate safety and school handover.
