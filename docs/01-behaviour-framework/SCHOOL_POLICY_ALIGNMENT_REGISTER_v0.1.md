## 1. Pedagogical foundation

The school policy combines clear rules with a warm, person-centred and restorative approach. The regulations explicitly call for clear human boundaries, understandable explanations, positive confirmation, additional opportunities for vulnerable pupils, dialogue and restorative methods.

### KlasKompas consequence

KlasKompas must not become a punishment counter. Its default order is:

```text
prevent → redirect → clarify → restore → document → consult/escalate
```

The system must support:

- clear and observable expectations;
- preventive teacher actions;
- short, calm correction;
- opportunities for self-correction;
- restorative follow-up;
- formal school measures only through the authorised school route.

## 3. Confirmed design boundaries

### 3.1 Classroom actions that can be designed now

The following can be designed as ordinary classroom management rather than formal school sanctions:

- non-verbal redirection;
- short verbal reminder;
- restating the routine or expectation;
- assigning or changing a seat;
- temporarily switching a pupil from group work to individual work;
- requiring the phone to be placed in the classroom pouch;
- closing a device or switching to a non-digital version of the task;
- repeating an entry, transition, clean-up or material routine;
- a short factual conversation after the lesson;
- documenting observable behaviour and creating a follow-up task.

### 3.2 Actions that remain behind a school gate

- official agenda/Smartschool warning classified as an order measure;
- punishment work as an order measure;
- detention or study detention;
- material service as a formal measure;
- temporary removal from lessons;
- behavioural contract;
- formal restorative procedure;
- disciplinary procedure, suspension or exclusion;
- official financial recovery for damage;
- official parent notification connected to a formal measure.

### 3.3 Excluded from an ordinary escalation counter

- violence or credible threats;
- bullying or sexual boundary violations;
- discriminatory or racist incidents;
- dangerous objects;
- drugs/alcohol;
- serious theft or deliberate serious damage;
- acute safety risks.

These incidents use the serious-incident route immediately.
