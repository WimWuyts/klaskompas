# Behaviour Matrix v0.1

**Status:** design draft based on the 2025-2026 school regulations  
**Scope:** ordinary classroom behaviour in English and Spanish lessons  
**Important:** the `Direct classroom response` column contains organisational responses, not automatically official school order measures.

## Fixed teacher language

### Reminder

> “You are [observable behaviour]. The expectation is [required action]. Do that now.”

### Documented classroom warning

> “This has continued after a reminder. I am recording a classroom warning. If it happens again in this lesson, [direct classroom response] follows.”

### Direct response

> “It happened again, so the announced classroom response now follows. We can discuss it after the lesson.”

### No debate during instruction

> “I will listen after the lesson. For now, follow the instruction.”

### Respectful disagreement

> “You may disagree. Say what you disagree with without insulting or threatening anyone.”

### Serious incident

> “Stop. Move to [safe instruction]. I am calling the school support route now.”

## Design rules

1. The first two corrections are normally not stored.
2. A documented classroom warning is a KlasKompas operational record, not automatically an official school order measure.
3. Direct responses must restore learning conditions and be possible immediately.
4. Formal punishment work, detention, study detention, official agenda notes and temporary removal remain behind the school-authority gate.
5. Genuine learning difficulty, technical failure or approved accommodation is not behaviour misconduct.
6. The system records an individual pupil only when individual behaviour was observed; it does not issue collective class penalties.
7. Serious incidents never wait for a numerical threshold.
